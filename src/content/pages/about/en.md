I'm a Lead AI Engineer at Yellow Tech, in Milan. The technical and architectural decisions behind the AI solutions we build for clients go through me, from feasibility to production. Alongside client work I look after the team's engineering method, the conventions and documentation that make the way we work repeatable, and the internal platforms we use every day.

I got here by a non-linear road. In 2021 I spent a year in one of the first Italian degree programmes in Artificial Intelligence, in Pavia, before AI Engineer was a job title, then chose to work. I taught kids to code, tried my hand at an EdTech startup with Let's Code Italia, later renamed [Ethicode](/en/projects/ethicode), whose platform I designed and built end to end, and from 2023 I worked in a software house, with freelance projects alongside.

The rest of my time goes into building my own things, which you'll find among the projects, and into figuring out which new AI ideas actually hold up in production. How I work with agents is written down in the [method](/en/method). I hold the Claude Certified Architect - Foundations certification from Anthropic.
