title: "Chess, video, a synth and local models"
---

November was the month of new projects. In [darwin-chess](/en/projects/darwin-chess) a cheap agent evolves a chess engine on its own, judged by games it cannot touch: you can watch it live. [Motio](/en/projects/motio) is the framework I now make my project videos with, and [piezo](/en/projects/piezo) a modular synth to build together with an agent. On [local models](/en/projects/local-llm-experiments) I am measuring how to run a large model without it taking the whole Mac. Meanwhile [Nexus](/en/projects/nexus) is becoming multi-user.
