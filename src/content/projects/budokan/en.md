The website of the Bu Do Kan sports club. The gym updates classes, schedules, instructors and the gallery on its own through Sanity, and anyone who wants to try a discipline books a free lesson straight from the site.

SvelteKit and TypeScript on the frontend, Sanity as the headless CMS.
