PumpGotchi is a digital pet living on Pump.fun, whose vital energy depends entirely on the trading of its associated token. It leverages the live streaming functionality to create a real-time interactive experience: not just a speculative token, but a true digital creature that reacts to market actions. The community must collaborate to keep it alive.

Four life stages tied to market cap: Egg ($0-10k), Hatchling ($10k-100k) with hunger and happiness active, Adult ($100k-500k) with new animations, Legend ($500k+) with golden aura. Psychological loop based on collective responsibility, emotional FOMO and trading gamification. Every transaction has immediate visible impact, creating natural viral content.
