The idea is the opposite of a feed: a dashboard that every day reads markets, crypto, news, weather and geopolitics, gives each signal a relevance score and shows me ten at most.

An LLM classifies, groups by topic and drops the noise; I only read what clears the threshold. It is related to Flux: there I read what I choose, here something chooses for me what is worth knowing.
