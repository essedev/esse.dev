Plannerinator is a comprehensive productivity platform integrating task management with priorities and deadlines, project organization with hierarchies, rich text note system, event and appointment tracking, customizable collections (movies, books, recipes, tools), habit and metrics counters, and universal tagging system to categorize all content.
