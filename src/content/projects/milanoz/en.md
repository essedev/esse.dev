Milan is split into 88 NIL, its local identity areas. The idea is a map where each one opens up and tells you what it is like: how many people live there, income, house prices, how much green space there is and how hot it gets in summer, plus places with a story and a piece on what living there is like.

There is a first prototype: a Python ETL pipeline that downloads and prepares the city's open data, and a frontend with a multi-layer map.
