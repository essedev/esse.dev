Measure first, then decide what to say, and take a wrong conclusion back in writing.

With agents that write in seconds, the work moves to verification: an agent goes as fast as the environment that checks it, and I trust it as much as I trust that environment. So I build it first, and out of its reach.

Tests, reviews and real numbers. In my projects the scope of what I measured, and of what I did not, is written next to the results.
