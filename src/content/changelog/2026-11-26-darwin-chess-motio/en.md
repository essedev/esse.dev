---
title: "Three new projects, two in the showcase"
---

[darwin-chess](/en/projects/darwin-chess), an agent that evolves a chess engine on its own, and [Motio](/en/projects/motio), the framework for making videos with React, have arrived: they enter the showcase in place of pgbee and Zeno, which stay among the [projects](/en/projects). There is also [piezo](/en/projects/piezo), a modular synth to build together with an agent, and [Local LLM Experiments](/en/projects/local-llm-experiments) has a new chapter on a RAM cap for large models.
