title: "Three new projects, and the entries brought up to date"
---

[darwin-chess](/en/projects/darwin-chess), an agent that evolves a chess engine on its own, and [Motio](/en/projects/motio), the framework for making videos with React, have arrived: they enter the showcase in place of pgbee and Zeno, which stay among the [projects](/en/projects). There is also [piezo](/en/projects/piezo), a modular synth to build together with an agent. [Local LLM Experiments](/en/projects/local-llm-experiments) is rewritten, with a new chapter on a RAM cap for large models, and the entries of Nexus, Floppy, Varco, Relay, Zeno and Copilota say where they stand now. The [Now](/en/now) page is up to date, and the method note on [measuring](/en/method/measure) says how I verify the agents' work.
