title: "Writing is back, and the agent has a new filter"
---

Writing returns with two articles: [The orange binder](/en/writing/the-orange-binder) and [What happens when you write to my agent](/en/writing/what-happens-when-you-write-to-my-agent). Every message to the [agent](/en/agent) goes through a small model that decides whether it is on topic, and I changed that too: I tested seven on the messages I keep for this, and wrong decisions drop from 4 to 1 out of 79, attacks that slip through from 3 to 1 out of 19.
